HOW WE MAKE IT HAPPEN

The slow fashion pledge.

We reject high-volume fast fashion waste. Every MyStyleZ dress is designed to outlast short-lived algorithm trends and stay in your rotation for years.

01

Micro-Batch Drops

We produce in limited runs of 150 pieces per style. Zero warehouse surplus, zero discount dumping, and zero landfill waste.

02

Regenerative Silks

Our cupro and plant-based viscoses come from closed-loop facilities that recycle 98% of water and non-toxic processing solvents.

03

Fair Atelier Living Wages

Crafted exclusively in certified, woman-owned tailoring studios in Florence and Jaipur with transparent compensation well above local minimums.
